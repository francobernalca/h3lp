About h3lp

h3lp is a free signpost for Ontario, made by Franco Bernal. We don’t run any of these services. We point you to trusted Ontario and Canada-wide organizations so you can reach real help directly. Every number was checked by hand; confirm local details by calling 211.

In immediate danger? Call 911.

Privacy

No account, no cookies, no tracking. What you type stays on this device. If you choose a city, h3lp asks Open-Meteo for that city’s weather forecast; nothing about you is sent. After your first visit, h3lp works without a connection.

To leave fast, tap Exit at the top, or press Shift 3 times. Quick exit does not clear your history. Use a private window if someone checks your phone.
